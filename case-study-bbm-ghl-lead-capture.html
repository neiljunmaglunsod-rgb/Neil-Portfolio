Business problem

Enquiries needed answers and a place in the CRM

People message the BBM Bookkeeping page about monthly bookkeeping, BIR filing, payroll and business registration. They need a quick answer in their own language, and their details need to reach the CRM so someone can follow up.

Fees and tax outcomes also depend on each client's records, so an assistant must not quote prices or make promises.

Automation solution

An assistant with clear limits, connected to the CRM

The assistant replies in Bisaya, Tagalog, English or Taglish, answers first and then asks for one detail at a time. Once it has what it needs, it shares a link for booking a free consultation. Lead details go to GoHighLevel through its API.

Workflow architecture

How it works

1

Messenger webhook

n8n verifies the Facebook webhook, responds right away and keeps only real customer messages.

2

AI agent

An OpenAI model with short conversation memory writes the reply and returns structured lead fields.

3

Lead verification

A code step checks that the name, phone and email appear in what the customer actually typed. Details that don't match are not saved as a lead.

4

GoHighLevel

The contact is created or updated, tagged by business and service, and new contacts get a note with the lead details.

Engineering details

Build details

Guardrails in the prompt

The assistant never quotes fees, guarantees a BIR outcome or accepts TINs, passwords, OTPs or bank details.

Human handoff

BIR notices, audits, urgent deadlines, complaints and requests for a person are passed to the bookkeeper.

Blocks made-up details

The verification step exists because an AI model can fill empty fields with sample names. Those no longer reach the CRM.

Phone formatting

Local numbers starting with 0 are converted to the +63 format before they are sent to GoHighLevel.
