About Me

Automation built around real business operations

I'm Neil Jun Maglunsod, an AI automation and GoHighLevel specialist in Surigao City, Philippines, with a BS in Electronics Engineering. I build the workflows behind enquiries, bookings and follow-up, mostly in n8n and GoHighLevel.

My work comes from real small-business operations: an apartelle, a bookkeeping practice and a manpower agency. That means handling mixed Bisaya, Tagalog and English messages, incomplete details and the cases where an AI reply needs a safeguard.

For custom tools, I build web apps through AI-assisted development with Claude Code. I state what is live and what is still in progress, so you can judge the work as it really is.
