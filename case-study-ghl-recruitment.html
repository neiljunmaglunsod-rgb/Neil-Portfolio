GHL Recruitment Automation

Colorado General Services — Surigao City, Philippines

A Facebook Messenger assistant for a manpower and janitorial agency. It answers job seekers in their own language, explains open positions and requirements, and sends every applicant to a GoHighLevel application form, so applications reach the CRM instead of staying in chat threads.

Built in n8n for the agency's Facebook page and GoHighLevel account

Business problem

Applicants asked on Messenger, and applications got lost there

The agency recruits company drivers and heavy equipment operators, and most applicants first contact it through its Facebook page. The same questions arrive every day, such as "Hiring pa mo?", "Unsa'y requirements?" and "Unsaon pag-apply?", in three languages and often outside office hours.

Answering by hand took staff time. Applicants who were told to send a resume by chat left their details buried in Messenger, with no single list of who had applied.

Automation solution

An AI front desk that hands applicants to the CRM

An n8n workflow answers every Messenger enquiry with an OpenAI agent that works from a knowledge base document the agency maintains. Whenever someone shows interest in applying, the assistant always shares the GoHighLevel application form. Each submission is stored as a contact in the CRM.

Wants to apply

Requirements + GHL form

Lists the documents needed and links the GoHighLevel application form. It never asks applicants to send a resume in chat.

Client or quotation

Routed to the office

Service and quotation requests are pointed to the office contact details instead of being answered by the AI.

Unknown question

Passed to staff

If the knowledge base has no answer, the assistant says staff will follow up rather than guessing.

Workflow architecture

How it works

1

Messenger webhook

Each new page message reaches an n8n webhook. An If node answers Meta's webhook verification request.

2

Knowledge base

A Google Docs node loads open positions, requirements and office details, so staff can update answers without editing the workflow.

3

AI agent

An n8n AI Agent with an OpenAI chat model and per-sender memory replies in the applicant's language and applies the recruitment rules.

4

Reply and GoHighLevel form

An HTTP request sends the reply through the Facebook Graph API. Applicants complete the GoHighLevel form, which creates the contact in the CRM.

Steps follow the node order in the exported n8n workflow.

Engineering details

Guardrails for recruitment

No promises the agency can't keep

The prompt forbids promising a job, guaranteeing a salary or inventing details. Salary questions get the agency's own policy wording.

Knowledge base first

The agent answers from the Google Doc first and falls back to fixed company information, which keeps answers consistent.

Strict application-link rule

A priority rule makes sure the form link appears whenever application intent is detected, in English, Tagalog or Bisaya.

Same language back

The assistant replies in the language the applicant used and only switches if they do.
